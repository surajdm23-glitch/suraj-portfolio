UX meets the Last Mile

Designing from the Doorstep

Daddera Suraj · Locus internship

During my internship at Locus, my design and product team did something a little different. I stepped into the shoes of a delivery rider and executed actual doorstep deliveries, going out on the ground and using the LOTR application (the delivery rider application), simulating a B2C and B2B client flow.

“We didn’t just use the app — we lived it.”

To truly design for someone, you have to be them. Face the pressure of traffic, navigate tricky addresses, handle customers, and juggle app interactions on the go.

What I gained

  • First-hand exposure to rider pain points
  • A deeper lens on empathy-driven design
  • Realisation of how small UX tweaks impact flow, effort, and motivation
  • Insights that go beyond the screen, into mindset, environment, and behaviour

The day, as a comic

I even turned this journey into a comic, because sometimes visual stories tell ground truths better than words.

One takeaway for every aspiring designer

Design starts on the ground, not just on a screen.

Before jumping into secondary research or wireframes, talk to your users, observe them, and live their journey. No amount of internet research can replace primary, contextual, and participatory research.

Walking in your user’s shoes, quite literally, builds unmatched empathy, clarity, and intuition. It helps you design not just for usability, but for motivation, emotion, and behaviour.

Don’t hesitate to get your hands dirty. That’s where real UX insight begins.

A huge shoutout to the Locus design and product team for supporting this bold exploration. This experience taught me more about product design than any spec or screen ever could.
